"It's going away soon so we don't have to do xyz...."

Things take longer than you'd expect. "going away soon" means very different things to different people and usually ignores things that are important somehow. For example, I remember hearing "that server is going away soon" for 2 years at one job. All along, the server was missing password updates, security updates, monitoring updates. People say "going away soon" when they want to avoid doing something, but the consequences and tech debt adds up much more quickly than expected.

I started responding with a clarifying question to get a sense of soon "About how long? a week, a month?". Depending on the context, I have an internal threshold where I let it go. For me, 6 weeks. If "soon" is more than 6 weeks, then my own nervous system needs to still manage it. Most folks can estimate out 6 weeks pretty well. So their response will be accurate enough. This all depends on the company/team/context/etc.
